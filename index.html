04How the Discussion Starts

Start with the requirement

  1. 01

    Share your requirement

    Tell us the product category, estimated quantity and any specifications, packaging or destination details already available.

  2. 02

    Requirement review

    PT Permata Bara Globalindo reviews the information and identifies what needs to be clarified before the next commercial discussion.

  3. 03

    Discuss feasibility and next steps

    Relevant supply and commercial feasibility can then be discussed based on the requirement and information available at that stage.

  4. 04

    Continue through direct business coordination

    If there is a basis to proceed, the next applicable commercial step is coordinated directly with the buyer.
